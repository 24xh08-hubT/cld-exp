PROCEDURE

1. Open web browser.
2. Go to AWS signup page.
3. Enter email address.
4. Enter AWS account name.
5. Click “Verify Email Address”.
6. Enter verification code.
7. Click “Verify”.
8. Enter password.
9. Confirm password.
10. Click “Continue”.
11. Select account type as Personal.
12. Enter personal details.
13. Enter phone number.
14. Enter address details.
15. Click “Continue”.
16. Enter billing information.
17. Click “Verify and Continue”.
18. Enter mobile number.
19. Enter OTP received.
20. Select support plan as Basic.
21. Complete account creation.
